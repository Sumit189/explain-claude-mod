How flight radar apps track planes

Planes broadcast where they are on 1090 MHz. Thousands of cheap ground receivers listen, forward the messages to a server, and the server draws the map you see.

The pipeline

  1. Transponder broadcasts about twice a second, unencrypted.
  2. Receiver (antenna plus a small computer, often run by volunteers) decodes it. Range is about 250–400 km, line of sight.
  3. Server merges feeds, removes duplicates, and computes MLAT.
  4. App gets updates every few seconds and adds flight schedules and airline data.
